Ario Sadafi

I am currently a Health AI Fellow at the Computational Health Center at Helmholtz Munich. My research focuses on machine learning for medical image analysis and I am interested in using AI for disease diagnosis and clinical support.

I completed my Ph.D. at the Institute of AI for Health at Helmholtz Munich and Chair of Computer Aided Medical Procedures at Technical University of Munich, under the supervision of Carsten Marr and Nassir Navab.
